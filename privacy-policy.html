Privacy Policy

RAJ Walkie Talkie

Last updated: October 1, 2026

RAJ Walkie Talkie ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private communication application designed to provide push-to-talk voice communication between users in an office or group.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, RAJ Walkie Talkie may process information required to provide its services, including:

  • Email address used for account authentication.
  • Account information required to create or join an office/group.
  • Office or group information created or joined by the user.
  • Voice/microphone input when the Push-to-Talk feature is actively used.
  • Technical information required for communication and app operation.

2. Microphone Permission

The app requires microphone access to provide its Push-to-Talk voice communication feature.

Microphone access is used when the user activates the voice communication feature. The app does not require microphone access for unrelated purposes.

3. Voice Communication

RAJ Walkie Talkie uses real-time communication technology to transmit voice between participating users.

Voice communication is used only to provide the application's communication service.

4. Account Information

Users may need to sign in to use the application. Account authentication information is processed to authenticate users and provide access to the application's features.

5. Office and Group Information

Information associated with offices or groups may be stored so that users can create, join, and use communication groups.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Supabase for authentication, database services, and real-time communication infrastructure.

These services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information used by the application from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's features, maintain accounts, operate offices/groups, and meet applicable legal or security requirements.

If you want information associated with your account to be deleted, contact us using the email address provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

RAJ Walkie Talkie is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.
