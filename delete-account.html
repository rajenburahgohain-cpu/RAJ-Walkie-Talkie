Account & Data Deletion Request

Delete Your Account

If you have an account with RAJ Walkie Talkie and want your account and associated personal data deleted, you can submit a deletion request using the method below.

Important: Please send the request from the email address associated with your RAJ Walkie Talkie account so that we can verify the request.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Tap the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Enter the email address associated with your RAJ Walkie Talkie account.
  3. In the email, write: Delete my RAJ Walkie Talkie account.
  4. Send the request.
Account deletion request

We will use the request to verify the account and process the deletion of the account and associated data according to our data retention and privacy practices.

Request Account Deletion

What Will Be Deleted?

We will process the deletion request for the RAJ Walkie Talkie account and associated user data that is eligible for deletion.

Certain information may be retained only where retention is required by applicable law, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate retention requirements. Any such retention will be handled according to the applicable privacy policy.

Need Help?

If you have a question about account deletion or your data, contact the RAJ Walkie Talkie support address listed on Google Play.
